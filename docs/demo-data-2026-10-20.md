## How the data was made

- Patients, Patient Information, consultations, BPG and oral visits, echoes, INR, procedures, pregnancy and hospital admission records went in through the OpenMRS REST API, dated back as far as 410 days so that adherence, overdue, lost to follow-up and five-month silences are real computed results, not hand-set values.
- Three steps were done in the browser with Playwright so the real forms and workspaces were exercised: Hellen Akot was registered through the registration form and her first BPG recorded through the BPG Delivery form; Agnes Achola was enrolled in a study from her chart's Research participation page; Rose Atim was marked deceased from the chart's Actions menu. The two studies, "RHD Genetics Cohort" and "Heart Health Cohort", were created through Admin > Studies.
- A full refresh (`POST /actcore/refresh`, or Admin > Flags and adherence > Refresh now) was run at the end. The nightly task keeps the data current from here.

## What the demo illustrates, by topic

- **The full status range on one list.** Due for prophylaxis has 11 rows: eight Overdue (Zara, Kato, Joan, Isaac, Faith, Sarah, Abebe, Grace, oldest first), Agnes Due today, Brian and Christine Due soon. The rest of the registry is Covered or has no prescription.
- **Never started.** Zara (oral) and Kato (BPG) show what the first due date rules do when nothing has ever been given: both fall due from the prescription, become overdue, and after five months lost to follow-up.
- **Adherence from history.** Abebe 87%, Daniel 71%, Sarah 50%, Isaac 15% and Rose 29% are computed from dose timing, not typed in. Daniel's late doses each carry a reason, so the Prophylaxis page explains the 71%.

## Observations worth a ticket

These came out of reading the data back and are not scenario design:

1. **A deceased patient keeps her registry enrolment, flags and next steps.** Rose's RHD Registry programme enrolment has no completion date after Mark patient deceased, she still carries the RHD lost to follow-up flag after the refresh, and the chart still asks every role to give the overdue injection. Only the Due for prophylaxis report excludes her; the cascade still counts her as Active. The study enrolment, by contrast, closed correctly.
2. **Consent No only adds an asterisk.** Noah is registered with consent No; the registry marks his name with an asterisk and otherwise tracks, lists and counts him exactly like a consenting patient. If the registry rule is that refused consent keeps a patient off the lists or raises a flag, nothing implements it.
3. **Care cascade Adherent (80%+) is taller than Covered today** (12 against 11), because Adherent counts overdue patients with a good history. This is issue #150.
4. **A fixed cause of death list.** Mark patient deceased offers six coded causes (traumatic injury, neoplasm, infectious disease, unnatural death, unknown, other) and a free-text box. Heart failure, the registry's main cause, is only reachable as Other with text; a cause-of-death concept set for ACT would be better for the mortality reports.
5. **A death recorded on the Procedures form does not mark the person deceased** (Isaac). The flag covers this, and the flag's action opens Mark patient deceased; the demo keeps Isaac in that state on purpose so the flag can be shown.
